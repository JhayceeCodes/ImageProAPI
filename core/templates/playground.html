Request notes

  • The backend expects multipart form data with original_image and operations.
  • The operations field must be a JSON array, even if you only choose one operation.
  • Anonymous uploads are allowed, but they are limited to 2 operations and smaller files.
  • Authenticated requests use a Bearer token and get higher limits.
